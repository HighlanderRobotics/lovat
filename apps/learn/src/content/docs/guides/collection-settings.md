--- 
title: Lovat Collection Settings
description: All of Lovat Collection's settings and how to use them.
---

Open settings by tapping the gear icon on the home screen.

Here you can set the field orientation based on which side of the field you're on. Select auspicious for blue on the left, and sinister for red on the left. You can also select the tournament you're at. This allows you to view your scouter schedule, which makes scouting much easier. 

Enable training mode if you are testing the app or training scouters and want to use it without uploading data. It will disable the submit button in the post match screen and force you to discard any data you record.

At the bottom of the page you can delete all settings and stored data and reset the app to its initial state.
